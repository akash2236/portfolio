RESUME PLACEMENT INSTRUCTIONS
============================
Your verified resume is placed at:
public/resume.pdf

The "Download Resume" buttons across the portfolio navigation, hero section, and interactive terminal directly link to this file.
When updating your resume in the future:
1. Export your updated resume as a PDF file.
2. Name the file exactly 'resume.pdf'.
3. Replace the existing 'public/resume.pdf' file with your new document.
4. Deploy the updated project to Vercel or your hosting provider.
